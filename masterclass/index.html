The flagship

Masterclass Business Narratives

Turn a complex subject into a strong narrative, report, presentation or speech — working on your own real case, with individual expert feedback.

The problem it solves.

Meetings take too much time. Information and presentations are too complex. Teams are not fully aligned, and decisions stay vague. And it is getting worse: more information, more complexity, more pressure and more AI-generated content — with less time, less attention and less critical thinking to process it.

Our research measured it: managers spend 25% of their time on presentations, yet 76% of presentations have no clear added value. What makes the difference is not the slides or the delivery — it is the narrative: the content and its structure.

What you learn.

The theory is kept minimal, but rests on a robust scientific foundation: the limitations of the human mind, thinking and decision processes, the theory of influence, intuitive versus conscious thinking, auditive and visual perception.

How it works: a blended journey.

Maximal learning with minimal work disruption — 24 hours spread over 8 weeks, with spacing designed for durable learning.

Interactive learning

4 live workshops

Four online workshops of 2 hours (or face-to-face sessions) to deepen your understanding, get interactive feedback and make the learning actionable with colleagues — plus an optional refresher module 6–12 months later.

Self-paced learning

40 short video lectures

Theory and tips in videos of 5–10 minutes — 12 core, 28 optional — with assignments, case studies, polls and quizzes. Learn when it suits your agenda.

Real-life application

Your own case

You work throughout on a real narrative or presentation from your own job — by default a 10-minute management presentation or pitch, prepared in pairs.

Support

Feedback & hotline

Individual written feedback from your teacher, a support hotline for 16 weeks, an online learning guide with all materials, and the book included.

Customisable for your circumstances.

  • Group size — default 12 participants; formats possible from 6 to 48.
  • Classroom vs online — default 4 online sessions of 2 hours; face-to-face or hybrid on request.
  • The cases — number, length and complexity of the narratives participants work on.
  • Support level — feedback depth and frequency, hotline duration.
