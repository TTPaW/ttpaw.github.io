Format: how it is engineered.

  • The full learning chain — theory → tools → guided practice → real-world application → feedback and support.
  • Multi-channel by design — 40 short videos, instructor-led sessions, written feedback, instructor support and peer feedback: each channel matched to a learning objective, participant preference and time constraint.
  • Balanced repetition — video, workshops, book and reminders revisit the same core across channels for durable retention.
  • Progressive spacing — learning moments are spaced further apart as the journey progresses (1 week → 2–3 weeks → 4–5 weeks), the pattern memory research favours.
  • Calibrated durations — videos 5–10 minutes, workshops 2 hours, face-to-face sessions 2×3 hours, exercises max 20 minutes: matched to real attention spans.
  • Continuity — a central hub as single reference, reminders, and catch-up support for unforeseen absence.
  • Flexible scale — group sizes from 6 to 48 with format adaptations; paired work as the default working configuration.

Delivery: what we take off your plate.
