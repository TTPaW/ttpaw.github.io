Speaking to inspire: New programme at Vlerick Business School

Vlerick Business School recognizes the importance of presentations for business in general and business leaders in particular. The new programme “Speaking to inspire” is much more than just a presentation skills programme. It’s about a new way of thinking for senior business leaders in their approach to communication and presentation. The 4-day programme is built around the TLSM method, conceived by Ed Gruwez and will be lead by Prof Peter De Prins and Ed Gruwez jointly.
