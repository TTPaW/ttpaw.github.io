Unique TLSM workshop in London

Presentation Skills Training
More impact in less time

TLSM is the solution for effective business presentations. Find out why here.

On Sept 22nd Ed Gruwez, will explain the basics of TLSM:

  • how the human brain processes information with fast & slow thinking
  • how to structure logic and ideas to create more impact in less time
  • how to turn content into a an inspiring story
  • how to keep balance between objective argumentation and subjective storytelling

Location: Flanders House, 1A Cavendish Square, London W1G 0LD

Speaker : Ed Gruwez  author of Presentation Thinking & Design and To The Point Presentations

Date: Thursday  22 September 2016 – 9.30am-5pm

Fee: £ 375 including the book ‘Presentation Thinking & Design’ (exceptional one-time offer with the support of BLCC)
