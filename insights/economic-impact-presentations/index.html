The economic impact of presentations

According to a survey with 273 managers in large organizations, the economic impact of presentations is not to be underestimated: presentations directly cause a waste of 8 to 17% of people’s time.

  • On average, employees spend more than 9 hours a week giving, attending or preparing presentations.
  • 45% of all presentations are too long or much too long.
  • In 47% of the cases, the attendees state they have learned little or nothing.
  • Only 24% of all presentations actually produce a marked effect
