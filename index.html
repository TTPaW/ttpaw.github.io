The method

TLSM — one framework from thought to message.

Most training fixes the surface: slides and delivery. TLSM works where the value is created — the quality of the thinking and the structure of the narrative.

  1. T

    Thinking · 

    understand your audience, clarify your purpose.

  2. L

    Logic · 

    content that is correct, coherent, concise — and unbiased.

  3. S

    Story · 

    a narrative that is attractive, influential and memorable.

  4. M

    Media · 

    slides and documents that are easy to understand.
