Working alongside you

Projects: when training isn't enough.

Training builds the capability. But when something really matters — a strategy the whole organisation must understand, a board presentation that has to land, reporting that no longer serves its readers — we go further than training: we join your team and help do the actual work.

What a project looks like.

Strategy

Articulating strategy at scale

Helping a management team agree on what the strategy really is, expressing it as one coherent narrative, and cascading it so the whole organisation can tell the same story. Example: aligning the management teams of three pharmaceutical companies on a joint strategy after an M&A.

Reporting

Restructuring executive reporting

Redesigning what an executive team reads and presents: which reports exist, what question each answers, how they are structured and designed. Example: redesigning the board documentation of an international manufacturing company.

High-stakes support

Help with the work itself

A strategic presentation, an executive summary, a decisive document: we help you prepare and sharpen it — or design the communication and presentation for you. Your content, our cognitive precision, one deadline.

Processes

Insight reporting, end to end

Rebuilding how an organisation turns analysis into insight: the deliverables, the process, the templates and the skills. Example: analysing and redesigning reporting processes for a leading market-research company facing AI-driven competition.

How we work.

  • With your team, not instead of it — the capability stays when we leave.
  • Method-based — the same TLSM framework and cognitive principles as our training, applied to your material.
  • Combined with training where useful — projects often pair with a Masterclass or workshops so the new way of working sticks.
  • Honestly scoped — when a need falls outside our competence, we say so and point you to someone better placed.
