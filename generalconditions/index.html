9. Working conditions

The kind of work TTPI performs often involves irregular working times, international travel and longer-than-usual working days. TTPI consultants will do their utmost to accommodate the needs and wishes of the Client. However, it is of paramount importance that the workload remains reasonable and that consultants get regular and sufficient rest, to ensure the highest quality of service and out of respect for the consultants and their families. This implies:

  • travel across time zones is followed by recuperation time equal to half the time difference;
  • travel between 10 pm and 7 am is followed by recuperation time equal to half the travel time, cumulated with time-zone recuperation;
  • travel longer than 5 hours or during night time (10 pm – 7 am) is in Business Class; if the Client's policies do not allow Business Class, recuperation times are doubled;
  • the Client covers all travel and accommodation costs; accommodation, meals and travel are of standard business quality, with a 4-star hotel for trips longer than 5 days;
  • the Client pays a daily travel allowance according to the official per-diem rates referred to in clause 4;
  • travel time, waiting time and recuperation time are charged at 50% of the applicable consultant fee, unless agreed otherwise. Waiting time is time between travel, or time a consultant stays abroad between services;
  • for work during night time (10 pm – 7 am), on Sundays or on public holidays, fees are increased by 50%.
