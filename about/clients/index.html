Six sectors, six cases.

Public sector — UWV (Netherlands)

Helping policymakers decide

UWV, the Dutch social-security agency, struggled with overwhelming data and communication complexity in policy decisions. We trained key managers, then directors — enabling clear, impactful insights for ministers and decision-makers.

Financial sector — Ageas (global)

Future leaders who present with impact

In Ageas's annual leadership programme with Vlerick and the Center for Creative Leadership, participants present strategic proposals to the global executive committee. Our tailored masterclass equips them to think strategically and present convincingly.

Pharma — AstraZeneca (France)

Strategic alignment through reporting

Reporting to the global CEO and his leadership team had to drive strategic and investment decisions — with diverse audiences, high complexity and vast data. A tailored masterclass for the French leadership team refined how they report and present.

Technology — Barco (global)

Technical complexity, stronger narratives

Overly technical project reporting hindered management and client engagement. An adapted masterclass helped engineers distil technical data into clear, actionable narratives.

Heavy industry — Carmeuse (global)

Better management information

The world's second-largest lime producer needed better communication between remote experts, senior management and clients. We helped make highly technical presentations and executive summaries clear enough to decide on.

Research — Ipsos

Insight reporting in the AI era

Excessive analysis and cumbersome reports met clients demanding clear yet detailed insights — in a market being reshaped by AI. We analysed existing processes and redesigned how insights are reported.
