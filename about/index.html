About us

Deep in one thing. Wide in experience.

Our strength is deep expertise in ONE competence — cognitive precision in narratives, reporting and presentations — combined with wide experience across sectors and functions. Clients tell us no other supplier goes this deep on critical thinking for business narratives.

Edouard Gruwez.

Edouard combines an academic background in exact sciences, business management and corporate governance with top-management experience. For the last fifteen years he has focused on applying cognitive science to communication and decision-making.

He wrote several books on business narratives — including Presentation Thinking & Design (Financial Times Publishing) — teaches at business schools (elected best visiting professor at IE, with teaching-excellence awards 2017–19), and helps global companies become more impactful in strategy, communication and reporting.

PresenceNicosia · Antwerp · Paris · Madrid · Shanghai — supported by a small team that manages every programme end to end.
